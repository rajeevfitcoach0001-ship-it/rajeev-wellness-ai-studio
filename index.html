Samples

Output kaisa dikhta hai

Yeh illustrative examples hain jo tool generate karta hai — aap apni condition, audience aur offer ke hisaab se badal sakte hain.

Facebook post

"Crash diet se weight wapas kyun aata hai?"

Sirf 800 calorie khaakar 5 kilo kamana aasan lagta hai — par 90% logon ka weight 6 mahine mein wapas aa jaata hai. Isliye hum crash diet nahi, lifestyle dete hain. Aapka khana aapke ghar ka hi ho sakta hai. Plan bas sahi hona chahiye. Free trial day ke liye message karein.

Format: caption + CTA

Reel script

"PCOD mein nashta kya karein"

HOOK (0–3s): PCOD hai? Nashta skip mat kijiye. BODY (3–18s): Protein + fibre ka combo — besan chilla, paneer bhurji ya dahi-poha. Sugar aur maida kam. CTA (18–22s): Roz ke PCOD-friendly meal ideas ke liye follow karein.

Format: hook / body / CTA

Health tip

"Paani peene ka sahi time"

Khaane ke turant baad 1 glass paani digestion ko slow karta hai. Khaane se 30 minute pehle ya 45 minute baad — yeh behtar routine hai. Din bhar mein 8–10 glass, thoda-thoda karke.

Format: short tip
